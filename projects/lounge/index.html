The details,
taken care of.

Cinema, in your own collection.+

FFmpeg 8, D3D11VA hardware decoding, exact seeking, pitch-correct speed, and libass subtitles. Sidecar subtitles, chapters, resume, and Next up are built in.

LOCAL VIDEO / FFmpeg 8 + libass
The soundtrack stays with you.+

Browse tagged albums, artists, and songs. Queue, shuffle, or repeat. Original ambient music sets the home-screen mood and fades when something else plays.

YOUR FOLDERS / CONTINUOUS PLAYBACK
Your whole PC gets a front row.+

Your Start Menu, installed games, and chosen executables become tiles. Built-in web tiles open services through WebView2; your own accounts and subscriptions still apply.

APPS + GAMES / EMBEDDED WEB TILES
Even the practical things belong.+

Explore drives, move files, view pictures, change wallpaper, and rename with a controller keyboard. Every button can be remapped, including holds and combinations.

FILE EXPLORER / REMAPPABLE CONTROLS

Web tiles use your own accounts and subscriptions. Some protected video may need a full browser. Lounge does not supply a streaming catalogue.
