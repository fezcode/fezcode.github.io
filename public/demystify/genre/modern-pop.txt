what:
 Streaming changed pop's incentives: the first fifteen seconds decide whether a track is skipped, loudness normalisation removed the reward for brickwalled masters, and the album became a delivery vehicle for playlist placements. The music got quieter and more intimate as a result — Lana Del Rey's cinematic melancholy, The Weeknd's retro-synth reinvention, and a large field of bedroom-scale production that would have been unreleasable in 2005.

artists:
 *Lana Del Rey*, *The Weeknd*, Doja Cat, PinkPantheress, Florence + The Machine, Justin Timberlake, Santigold, Mark Ronson, Oh Wonder, Anna of the North, Seal and Enrique Iglesias as the older guard, and the sped-up-and-reverbed edit culture that now generates hits on its own.

trivia:
 PinkPantheress built her early catalogue on two-minute tracks made in a bedroom over drum-and-bass and UK garage breaks, released to TikTok before any label involvement — several were shorter than the average 1960s single. The "sped up + reverb" edit, once a piracy artefact, is now commissioned by labels and released officially alongside the original.

sonic:
 A single voice mixed very close and very forward, minimal instrumentation behind it, and a low end that is felt rather than heard. Tempos have fallen steadily since 2010. Song structures front-load the hook and often omit the bridge entirely; two-and-a-half minutes is now a normal length.

gear:
 Everything in-the-box — Logic and Ableton, Serum and Omnisphere, and stock plugin chains that are shared across the industry. Vocal production runs Auto-Tune or Melodyne as a matter of course, frequently as an audible texture rather than a correction, plus heavy parallel compression.

prod:
 Mastered for loudness normalisation rather than against it: because streaming platforms turn everything down to a common level, a quieter, more dynamic master now sounds _better_ than a crushed one, reversing twenty years of practice. Mixes are checked on phone speakers first. Arrangements leave the low-mid deliberately empty so the vocal reads on any device.
