## About

**Anatomy of a Fall** (*Anatomie d'une chute*) follows Sandra, a woman accused of killing her husband Samuel after he is found dead outside their home in the mountains. Their visually impaired son Daniel attends the trial, where his parents' private relationship becomes evidence in a public argument over what happened.

The courtroom investigation draws the marriage into focus: the question of how Samuel died becomes inseparable from competing accounts of their life together. [Film details and synopsis — Festival de Cannes](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/f/anatomie-d-une-chute/).
