# The Outer Worlds 2

The Outer Worlds 2 simply does not cut it. Everything about the game feels dated, lifeless, and frustrating to actually play.

Movement is stiff and clunky. You cannot even jump onto ledges that are clearly within arm's reach. That frustration carries right into the quests, which are nothing more than annoying, repetitive fetch errands. Instead of pulling you in, the world feels completely empty and dull. I kept waiting to feel immersed, and it never happened.

Combat does not save it either. The gunplay is so sluggish and unsatisfying that getting into a fight feels like a chore rather than fun. On top of that, the technical performance is a mess, the frame rate is all over the place and refuses to stay stable, making bad gunplay feel even worse. You hope the RPG elements and perks will at least make your character build exciting, but the perks end up feeling pointless and change almost nothing about how you play.

Honestly, enough is enough. Obsidian has been coasting on the goodwill of Fallout: New Vegas for way too long, and that excuse does not work anymore. They have not put out a truly great game since New Vegas, and The Outer Worlds 2 is officially the final straw for me.
