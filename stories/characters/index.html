Real Estate Developer

A drug-addicted veteran seeking revenge for his daughter Lenia. Scientist / Strategist

A brilliant strategist who secretly used Proxanian Seeds and Sansu Syrup to develop her formula. She sought to create a botanical neural network but never intended to create a pathogen. After demanding to see Patient Zero (Pellera Orlong), she realized Willare's mistake involved her secret Proxanian materials. She remains silent about the source to protect her own life and research.

Muatti

Scientist / Defector

A money-driven researcher who clawed his way out of the alleys into a white coat at St. Jude's. Promoted to study the Touch of Rose patients after his superior went mad, he became the first to measure the eleven-hour pulse from the inside—and to grasp its horror: the Rose-Marked are not broadcasting but answering a call beneath the mountain. He tried to destroy Project Glacier's data and was marked for death.

Muatti's brilliant, untouchable predecessor at St. Jude's. Prolonged exposure to the RedLink's Proxanian Echo shattered his mind; he was committed raving of twelve hearts beating as one drum and a black sun beneath the mountain. His breakdown opened the post that Muatti was promoted to fill.

Pugion

Prizefighter / Enforcer

An unbeatable fighter in the arenas of Glareach. After the men he had crossed murdered his wife and daughter in a staged "traffic accident," grief hollowed him out and they made him their weapon—pointing him at marked men in the ring and laundering the kills as sport. The crowd knows only the name "Pugion"; the husband and father who once owned the older name is gone.
