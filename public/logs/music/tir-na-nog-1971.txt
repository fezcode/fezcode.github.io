## About

**Tír Na NÓg** is the 1971 debut album of **Tír na nÓg**, the Irish folk duo of Leo O'Kelly and Sonny Condell, released on Chrysalis. The band takes its name from the Land of Youth of Irish mythology. The 2012 remaster runs 15 songs and 51 minutes, including two bonus tracks.
