## Setup

The library itself needs no accounts — launch Cogas and everything installed locally appears. The optional pieces each unlock one feature:

| To enable | Do this |
|---|---|
| Store, Deals, Wishlist | Get a free [IsThereAnyDeal key](https://isthereanydeal.com/apps/), paste it into **Settings**, hit *Test Key* |
| Extra critic scores | Get a free [RAWG key](https://rawg.io/apidocs) and paste it into **Settings** — optional, since Steam and Wikidata work without it |
| Steam owned games | Get a [Web API key](https://steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey), enter it with your SteamID64 or vanity name, then *Sync owned games*. Your Steam game details must be public. |
| GOG / Epic owned games | In **Settings**, open the login page, sign in, paste back the URL (GOG) or the JSON the page shows (Epic), *Connect*, then *Sync owned games* |

## Owned versus installed

Connecting Steam, GOG and Epic also shows games you own but have not installed. Ubisoft, Battle.net and EA publish no library API, so their games appear once installed — an honest limit rather than a silent gap.

## Non-store games

Add any executable — emulators, itch builds, DRM-free games — with optional cover art and launch arguments. They sit in the same grid as everything else.

## Where things live

Settings, the library index, cached art and the encrypted keys all live in `%APPDATA%\fezcode\cogas`. **Settings → Local Data** shows the exact path and opens the folder.
