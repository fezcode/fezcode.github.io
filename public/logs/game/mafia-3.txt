# Mafia III

Mafia III trades the made men for an outsider. Lincoln Clay comes home from Vietnam to 1968 New Bordeaux, finds his family murdered by the mob, and decides to tear the whole operation down, one racket at a time.

The documentary framing, with interviews and testimony cut between missions, gives the story real weight, and it confronts the racism of the era head on instead of using it as set dressing. Lincoln is one of the series' most compelling leads.

And the soundtrack: Creedence, Hendrix, the Stones, Johnny Cash. Driving through the bayou with the radio on is pure 1968.
