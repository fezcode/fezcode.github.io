**Eden** is the fifth track on **Blue Wonder Power Milk**, Hooverphonic's second album, released on 11 May 1998 and the band's first with Geike Arnaert on vocals. Written by Alex Callier and produced by the band with Mark Plati, it was released as a single in 1999 and reached number 12 on the Walloon chart.

## Cold

It is a cold song. Not sad, not angry, just cold: strings standing still in the room, a slow trip-hop beat, and Geike Arnaert singing as if from behind a window you cannot open. Nothing in it tries to warm you up, and it is beautiful precisely because of that distance.

## Straight to the Point

Three and a half minutes. No long intro, no build-up for the sake of it, no bridge that wanders off. It arrives, says what it came to say, and leaves.

## The French Horn

And then the French horn. It cuts through the strings with a sound that is somehow warm and lonely at the same time, and it is the most beautiful thing on the record. Every time it comes in, the whole song opens up for a moment before closing back into the cold.

**5/5.**
