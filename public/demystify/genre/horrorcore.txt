what:
 Horrorcore takes the horror-film register literally — the Geto Boys' "Mind Playing Tricks on Me" (1991) is the ancestor, Gravediggaz and Brotha Lynch Hung the codifiers. "Chopper" is a separate technical label for very high-density syllable delivery, associated with Midwest artists — Bone Thugs-n-Harmony, Twista, and above all *Tech N9ne*, who combines both.

artists:
 Geto Boys, Gravediggaz, Brotha Lynch Hung, Esham, Insane Clown Posse; *Tech N9ne* and the Strange Music roster; *Necro*, a Brooklyn producer-rapper who calls his own subgenre "death rap" and runs Psycho+Logical-Records. Necro's brother is Ill Bill of Non Phixion.

trivia:
 Tech N9ne co-founded *Strange Music* with businessman Travis O'Guin in 1999 after major-label deals fell through, and turned it into one of the most profitable independent labels in American music — _Forbes_ has repeatedly listed him among the highest-earning rappers despite near-zero radio play. The business model is relentless touring and direct merchandise sales, not streaming.

sonic:
 Minor-key, often orchestral or horror-organ loops with the drums mixed aggressively hot. Tech N9ne's delivery uses triplet and sextuplet subdivisions sustained across whole verses — the "chopper" style — with multi-tracked vocals doubling the fastest passages so the consonants stay legible. Necro sits at the opposite end: slow, heavy, sample-based, with the loop deliberately left crude.

gear:
 MPC and hardware samplers for Necro; Strange Music's in-house production (Seven, Michael "Seven" Summers) uses full DAW arrangement with orchestral libraries and live guitar. Vocal doubling is the key technique — often four or more takes stacked and gated tightly.

prod:
 Because the delivery is the product, the vocal recording is unusually careful: fast passages are punched in phrase by phrase and time-aligned so the stacks are sample-accurate. Beats are arranged to leave a rhythmic gap where the flow will land. Both artists master loud and mid-forward — this is music built for a van tour PA and a car system, not a streaming loudness target.
