# Mafia: The Old Country

The Old Country goes back to the beginning: early 1900s Sicily, long before Lost Heaven or Empire Bay. Enzo Favara climbs out of the sulfur mines and into the Torrisi family, and the story of how he earns his place is told with the series' trademark cinematic care.

Dropping the open world for a focused, linear structure was the right call. Every chapter is tight and purposeful, and the Sicilian countryside, the villages and the horseback rides between them are gorgeous.

It is a story about honor, family and what the old ways cost, and it feels like the roots of everything the series would become.
