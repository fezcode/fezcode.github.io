# Mafia

Mafia is where it all started, and it still holds up as one of the finest crime stories ever told in a game. Tommy Angelo's journey from an ordinary cab driver to a made man in the Salieri family is paced like a classic gangster film, rising slowly, then falling hard.

Lost Heaven is a love letter to 1930s America: Prohibition, rattling sedans, tommy guns and smoky bars. The city never feels like a playground; it feels like a place, and the story uses it with real purpose.

What stays with you is the ending. Few games commit to the consequences of the life their hero chose quite like this one.

"Mr. Salieri sends his regards."
