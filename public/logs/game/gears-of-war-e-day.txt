# Gears of War: E-Day

- **Category:** Game
- **Platform:** PC, Xbox Series X/S
- **Date:** 2026-11-14
- **Rating:** 5/5
- **Link:** [Gears of War: E-Day](https://www.gearsofwar.com/en-us/games/gears-of-war-eday/)

## What is Gears of War: E-Day

A Gears of War origin story following Marcus Fenix and Dominic Santiago through Emergence Day, fourteen years before the original game.

## Thoughts

I really loved the way this plays. The gameplay is wonderful, especially the movement and jumping. It feels great to play.

The graphics are the best I've ever seen. The visual quality is a huge part of what makes it stand out for me.

## Conclusion

Wonderful gameplay, movement I really enjoyed, and the best graphics I've ever seen. An easy **5/5**.
