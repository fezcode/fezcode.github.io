# gobake
The Go-native build orchestrator. Type-safe, dependency-free, baked fresh.
(version) 0.5.0

**gobake** replaces Makefiles, shell scripts, and yet-another-build-tool with a single, type-safe `Recipe.go` file. Inspired by `nob.h`, your build logic is written in Go and compiled on the fly. Version **0.5.0** moves `recipe.piml` parsing to go-piml v1.3.0, enforcing PIML spec v1.2.0: strict 2-space indentation, inline comments, quoted strings and duplicate-key errors.
