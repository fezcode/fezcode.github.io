## About

MachineGames' **Wolfenstein: The New Order** sends B.J. Blazkowicz into an alternate 1960s ruled by the Nazis. He joins a resistance fighting an enemy with overwhelming technological power, moving through occupied Europe and heavily fortified facilities in a campaign that combines stealth, enormous weapons, and desperate battles. [Game details — Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/201810/).

## My Notes

I love, **LOVE** this game. It belongs among the best games ever made, across any genre. That is how highly I rate what MachineGames did here.

The combat has tremendous force, and the people give that force a purpose. B.J. and the resistance make this world feel worth fighting for. The quieter moments give the campaign room to breathe, so the next burst of violence carries more than spectacle. There is anger here, but also tenderness and the stubborn belief that life can be better.

**5/5.** An all-time favorite, alongside *The Old Blood* and *The New Colossus*. These three mean a lot to me.
