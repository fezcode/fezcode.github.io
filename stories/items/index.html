Artifacts, curiosities, and tools of the trade.

Niva's Badge

Insignia

An intelligence officer's badge, now stained with the blood of a sewer worker. It represents the failure of the system to protect those who built it.

Alilberry Extract

Potion / Poison

A pearlescent grey mist derived from a rare herb, processed through dwarven glass and sung over by a mute bard. One drop cures; two drops kill.

Soul of Viyu

Artifact

A fragment of an ancient soul, pulsating with a faint, rhythmic darkness. It whispers to those who hold it, promising power at a terrible cost. It is the dark twin of the Sansu Syrup: it does not connect, it consumes. Its last known fragment—a cufflink kept by the Sultan of Butterflies—burned out carrying the truth through the RedLink.

Inspect Item

Corrigan's .38 Revolver

Weapon

A cold, heavy piece of steel that has seen better days. It is the only thing Corrigan trusts in a city full of liars and shadows. It fired the final shot in the Case of the Mirror Man.

Inspect Item

ᛉ

Dark Green Beret

Military Apparel

A wool beret with twenty-two silver stars stitched into the side by Lenia, one for every year she was alive before Glareach burned. It is the only thing Lorhud Krauper has left from his daughter, and he never takes it off.

Inspect Item
ᚦ
ᛉ

Chronometer of Ash

Tool / Artifact

A brass sphere powered by the ash of Glareach. It allows the user to relive exactly one hour of their past at the cost of a day of their future. The glass is unbreakable, ensuring the user can only observe, never intervene.

Inspect Item
ᚦ
ᛉ

Valeth's Notebook

Journal

A navy blue notebook with a single silver star on the cover. It contains the observations of a diplomat who sees too much, documenting the rot at the heart of Thornus. Recently stolen by Nex Orlong outside the Kosnak Estate.

Inspect Item
ᚦ
ᛉ

Touch of Rose

Disease / Mark

A devastating affliction that manifests as a crimson rose-leaf mark on the forehead. Over years it drains the victim's motor functions and speech, eventually leaving them in a vegetative state. Its only known cure is a rare drop of Alilberry. It rode the wind once, out of a bioreactor's exhaust; since then it passes only through the blood of the marked—Corshield's program learned to spread the mark by transfusion, but never to make it.

Government Medal for Science

Award

A heavy gold disc awarded for exceptional scientific achievement. To Avalia Krones, it is a symbol of her gilded cage and the government's attempt to buy her loyalty.

The Krones Formula

Scientific Data / Discovery

A revolutionary method for accelerating agricultural growth. While publicly used to end hunger, the government seeks its hidden 'weaponized' potential, which Avalia carefully conceals.

Leatherwave's Dried Fish

Food / Treat

Simple, salty snacks used to bribe the Council of Three. They represent the simple joys of a quiet life.

Sansu Syrup

Ancient Bio-Catalyst

An iridescent, vibrating fluid harvested from deep pockets within Proxan Mountain. Used by Proxanians for communal neural links. When pressurized with Viyu gas, it becomes a violent crimson 'Agitated' state.

Crimson Binder

Stabilizer / Pathogen

A viscous red concentrate used by Proxanians to bridge biological neural networks. Willare Frindjen mistakenly added this to the Krones Formula thinking it was nitrogen. Avalia realized this too late, knowing that the binder is now searching for a neural host in humans.

Inspect Item
ᚦ
ᛉ

Proxanian Seeds

Biological Hardware

Dormant seeds of extinct flora that grew on Proxan Mountain. They respond only to the 'Proxanian Echo' and were the true foundation of the Krones Formula.

Inspect Item
ᚦ
ᛉ

Proxanian Echo

Acoustic Artifact / Frequency

The background psychic frequency of the extinct Children of the Mountain—not a broadcast, as Corshield's reports claimed, but a call. Avalia Krones sought to harness it for her 'botanical network'; the 'Touch of Rose' victims were biologically compelled to answer it. It fell silent on the night the RedLink fell, when the Soul of Viyu poured down the link and met the caller.

Ash of Glareach

Catalyst / Fuel

Fine gray powder harvested from the burn-sites of the old city. It retains the 'chronological weight' of the fire, making it the only substance capable of powering a Chronometer or stabilizing a RedLink.

The Skulls in the Ceiling

Macabre Trophy

The remains of Penta Kosnak's parents, hung openly from the chandelier of the Kosnak Estate as "rebel" trophies. Their true identity is the secret: Wulf tells the world they were assassins, and tells his wife nothing at all.

Industrial Chemical

A standard agricultural supplement. The empty canister of Batch #4 stands in Avalia's lab as a reminder of the night Willare reached for the wrong shelf.
