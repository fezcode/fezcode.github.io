# Masala Chai

Masala chai is an unbeatable comfort drink—strong black tea simmered directly in whole milk with freshly crushed spices: green cardamom, ginger, cloves, cinnamon, and black pepper.

### Comparison: Chai Tea Latte & Sahlep

- **vs. Chai Tea Latte:** Commercial chai lattes usually rely on ultra-sweet syrups with diluted spice notes. Authentic masala chai is decocted from scratch, delivering a vibrant ginger bite, aromatic cardamom floral notes, and a robust tannic tea backbone without syrup heaviness.
- **vs. Sahlep:** Turkish sahlep is thick, velvety, and gentle with orchid root starch and surface cinnamon. Masala chai shares that deep winter warmth, but brings sharper, more invigorating spice heat (pepper + ginger) and a refreshing caffeine kick.

A warming, balanced masterpiece of spices and milk.

**Rating: 5/5**
