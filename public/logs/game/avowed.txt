# Avowed

These are first impressions, and they're already enough for a 5. I've put about three hours into Avowed and haven't touched the main quest yet, which says something on its own: the game keeps finding ways to pull me sideways.

## It looks alive

I'll start with the character animations, because they're great. Faces move the way faces should, and the world around them looks just as good.

Sometimes my character looks straight at the camera. It's beautiful and a little creepy at the same time.

![My character at the docks, looking straight into the camera](/images/logs/games/avowed/docks-camera.webp)

## Combat that never drags

The weapon play is good, and the variety of weapon types is even better. Fights feel swift. Nothing about them is boring, which is more than I can say for The Outer Worlds 2, Obsidian's other recent release.

Even the UI moves with you. Fire a gun and the whole screen shakes, HUD included. It's a small touch, and it makes everything feel far more immersive.

Mixing spells with gunpowder is extremely fun.

## Voices worth listening to

The sound design is fine, but the voice acting is almost perfect. Conversations are where the game earns your time.

## One complaint, for every game

Character creation should turn DLSS off. Every slider changes the face in front of you, DLSS can't keep up with those changes, and you end up judging your character through a smeared mess.

## So far

Three hours in, Avowed is everything The Outer Worlds 2 wasn't: it looks alive, it fights fast, and it sounds right. More once I actually start the story.
