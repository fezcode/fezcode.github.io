## About

**Wolfenstein II: The New Colossus** continues MachineGames' story of B.J. Blazkowicz, bringing the fight to Nazi-occupied America. B.J. works with the resistance to rally a revolution and reclaim his homeland. [Game details — Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/612880/).

## My Notes

I love, **LOVE** this game. MachineGames goes for enormous action, outrageous situations, and deeply personal stakes with a confidence that makes the whole thing exhilarating. Its defiance is infectious.

The characters give that defiance a human face. B.J. carries the fight forward, but the people around him make the resistance feel alive: angry, funny, damaged, and determined to have a future. The game's willingness to put those feelings beside its wildest action is a huge part of its identity.

**5/5.** Together with *The New Order* and *The Old Blood*, this is among the best gaming has to offer. All three belong on my list of the greatest games ever made, across every genre.
