Typewriter is a distraction-free text editor with the soul of a mechanical typewriter.

Built in C with SDL2 — fast, lightweight, cross-platform. Every keystroke clicks. Every carriage return clunks. The paper is warm, the cursor blinks, and the bell rings at column 80.

There is no sidebar, no minimap, no status bar full of extensions. There is a page, and the sound of typing on it.
