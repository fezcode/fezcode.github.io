## About

**Dawn FM** is The Weeknd's fifth studio album, released on 7 January 2022 by XO and Republic Records. It is built as a radio broadcast you tune into while stuck in traffic on the way out of this life: Jim Carrey is the host of 103.5 Dawn FM, calmly talking you through the tunnel towards the light, and Quincy Jones turns up halfway through to tell a story about his childhood.

Max Martin and Oneohtrix Point Never shape most of the sound, and it is pure 80s synth-pop and city-pop gloss: shimmering synths, four-on-the-floor disco, and Abel's voice floating on top of all of it. Sixteen tracks, fifty-two minutes, and every song runs straight into the next as if somebody is turning the dial.

## The Favorites

- **Take My Breath:** Five and a half minutes of disco that refuses to stop climbing.
- **Out of Time:** Smooth, aching city pop, and the most beautiful song on the record.
- **Is There Someone Else?:** Quiet and paranoid, sliding straight into "Starry Eyes" without a break.
- **Less than Zero:** The best way an album about dying could end, as a bright, upbeat pop song.
- **Phantom Regret by Jim:** Jim Carrey's closing poem over a slow, dissolving outro. You reach the light and it is calm.

## Why 5/5

Because it is a masterpiece. It is an album about death and purgatory, and it is somehow the most peaceful thing I have ever listened to. The concept holds from the first radio jingle to the last word of Jim Carrey's poem, the production is flawless, and there is not one track you would want to skip. You put it on, the traffic stops mattering, and for fifty-two minutes everything is quiet.
