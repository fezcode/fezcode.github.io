## About

**Wolfenstein: The Old Blood** is MachineGames' standalone prequel to *The New Order*, set in 1946. B.J. Blazkowicz infiltrates Castle Wolfenstein in search of intelligence that could help turn the war. The mission takes him onward to Wulfburg, where Nazi excavations threaten to awaken something ancient. Its eight chapters bring together infiltration, heavy combat, and supernatural horror. [Game details — Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/350080/).

## My Notes

I love this game just as fiercely as the other two. *The Old Blood* earns its place among my all-time favorites with its own character: the castle, the oppressive atmosphere, and that descent into the occult give the whole adventure a wonderfully sinister flavor.

The compact campaign makes its changing moods feel especially immediate. Sneaking through hostile spaces, breaking into open combat, and heading deeper into horror all belong to the same journey. MachineGames packs so much personality into it that its smaller scale feels like a strength.

**5/5.** One of the best games ever, measured against everything I play. My love for MachineGames' Wolfenstein absolutely includes this one.
