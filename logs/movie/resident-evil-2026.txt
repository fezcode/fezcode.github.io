## About

**Resident Evil** is Zach Cregger's survival-horror reboot of Capcom's franchise, co-written with Shay Hatten. Set during the T-virus outbreak in Raccoon City, it follows Bryan (Austin Abrams), a late-night medical courier trying to deliver an organ container to Raccoon General Hospital when an accident on the snowy outskirts drops him straight into a collapsing town overrun by mutating residents and corporate bio-weapons. Also starring Zach Cherry, Kali Reis, and Paul Walter Hauser. [Film details — IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt35538033/).

## My Notes

It felt just like the real original *Resident Evil* game—creeping through old houses and dark corners, looting and searching rooms, running into terrible dogs, finding green herbs scattered here and there, and genuine ammo management. Everything about the atmosphere and survival loop comes across like a faithful adaptation of the game, and I really liked it.

The protagonist curses a lot, which is quite understandable—anyone would in his shoes. Keeping the runtime around 90 minutes is also a big plus; they didn't stretch it out and kept it fun from start to finish.

**4/5.**
