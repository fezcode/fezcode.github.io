# The Suicide Squad (2021)

- **Category:** Movie
- **Director:** James Gunn
- **Release Date:** 2021-08-06
- **Rating:** 3/5
- **Link:** [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt6334354/)

Amanda Waller drops a squad of expendable supervillains on the island nation of Corto Maltese to destroy a Nazi-era lab and whatever is locked inside it. Most of them are not coming back.

## My Notes

I went in expecting it to be awful, and it turned out okay. James Gunn half-redeems the 2016 mess: it is loud, gory and happy to kill off anyone at any moment, and it is funny often enough to carry the slower stretches. Not great, but redeemable. It also gave us Peacemaker, which alone makes it worth it.
