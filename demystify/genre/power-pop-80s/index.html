4880S POWER POP & NEW WAVE

Sixties melodic craft played with eighties gloss and a very high male vocal

WHAT IT IS / WHEN

Power pop is Beatles/Who melodic writing with amplified guitars — Big Star and Cheap Trick in the 1970s, then a 1980s wave that absorbed new wave's keyboard textures and gated drums. The Outfield are its most anomalous success: three Londoners who were essentially ignored in Britain and sold millions in America.

SIGNIFICANT ARTISTS

Big Star, Cheap Trick, The Romantics, The Knack, Squeeze, The Cars; The Outfield — John Spinks (guitar, and sole songwriter), Tony Lewis (bass and that voice), Alan Jackman (drums).

TRIVIA

Play Deep (1985) went double platinum in the US while the band remained obscure at home; they were routinely assumed to be American, and the name itself was chosen because it sounded like baseball, a sport none of them followed. Tony Lewis' vocal sits in a range so high that almost every cover version transposes it down. Both Spinks (2014) and Lewis (2020) have since died.

SONIC SIGNATURE

Jangly, chorused electric guitar with a lot of high-mid presence, bass playing melodically and mixed loud, a gated snare with a short bright reverb, and a lead vocal at the very top of a tenor range doubled at the octave in the chorus. Harmony is major-key and diatonic with a surprise chord in the pre-chorus — the classic power-pop trick.

KEY GEAR

Rickenbacker and Telecaster guitars, Roland chorus (JC-120 amps or a Boss CE-2), Simmons electronic drum triggers layered under acoustic drums, Yamaha DX7 for the bell pads, and the era's defining processor: an AMS or Lexicon gated reverb on the snare.

RATIONALE & PRODUCTION

Recorded to 24-track analogue with heavy use of noise gates keyed to the snare — the gated-reverb sound was originally an accident from an SSL desk's reverse-talkback and became the decade's signature. Guitars are double-tracked and hard-panned; the bass is compressed heavily and given more high-mid than a modern mix would allow, which is why it cuts through so clearly on a small speaker.
