# Far Cry Primal

- **Category:** Game
- **Platform:** PC, PlayStation 4, Xbox One
- **Release Year:** 2016
- **Rating:** 2/5
- **Link:** [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/371660/Far_Cry_Primal/)

Far Cry Primal takes the established Far Cry open-world checklist and transplants it into the prehistoric valley of Oros (10,000 BCE). While the Stone Age concept is an interesting departure on paper, the execution falls completely flat.

### Why It Falls Short

- **Unsatisfying, Clunky Combat:** Stripping away guns leaves you with bows, clubs, and throwing spears. Instead of feeling visceral or intense, the melee combat feels floaty, unresponsive, and lacking in weight or tactical feedback.
- **Tedious Resource Grinding:** The loop leans heavily into endless material gathering and outpost clearing. Gathering wood, flint, and animal skins to upgrade huts feels like pure chore-work rather than meaningful survival.
- **Repetitive Loop & Shallow AI:** Beast taming is novel for the first hour, but quickly trivializes gameplay—commanding an oversized predator to clear camps removes what little tension existed.
- **Lifeless Progression:** The narrative, fictional proto-language, and repetitive tribal skirmishes fail to offer memorable character moments or compelling stakes.

### Verdict

It is simply not a good game. The moment-to-moment mechanics do not feel good to play, leaving a hollow, sluggish open-world formula without the mechanical satisfaction to back it up.

**Rating: 2/5**
