# A bar at the top of
every monitor you own

image: /images/projects/hisashi/banner.svg

Hisashi (庇) is Japanese for *eaves* — the protruding edge of a roof, the strip at the top of a structure. It is a plugin-driven top bar for Windows, built on .NET 9 and Avalonia, and every display gets its own with its own widgets, theme and behavior.
