03 / NO EXTRA BAGGAGE

Just you.
Your strip.
The connection.

01

From your PC to your light.

Bluetooth LE, directly to an ELK-BLEDOM strip. No cloud account, hub, or background service. Beetle reconnects to the strip it remembers.

02

Your soundtrack, in colour.

Music mode listens to what your PC is playing, through audio loopback. It never uses your microphone. Choose beat colours or let the wheel take over.

03

Good at staying out of the way.

Close the window and ambient lighting carries on from the tray. Your strip, colour, brightness, sample area, boost, and theme are remembered.

04

A precise little instrument.

Built for Windows with C#, .NET 10 and Avalonia 12. Ambient sampling uses the primary monitor. The strip is one colour zone, beautifully in sync.
