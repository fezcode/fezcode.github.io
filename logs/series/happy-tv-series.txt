## About

**Happy! (2017–2019)** adapts the Grant Morrison and Darick Robertson comic across two seasons and eighteen episodes. Nick Sax (Christopher Meloni) is an ex-detective turned hitman, working through a heart attack and most of a bottle, when a small blue winged unicorn appears and will not leave. Happy (Patton Oswalt) is the imaginary friend of a kidnapped girl who insists Nick is her father, and he is the only one who can see him.

## Why It Works

It survives on total commitment. The premise could be unbearable — a cartoon unicorn riding shotgun with a suicidal hitman — and it works because nobody involved treats it as a joke they are above. Meloni plays a man being physically dismantled across a season and never winks. Oswalt plays relentless sincerity against him without softening him.

Brian Taylor directs it the way he directed *Crank*: too fast, too loud, whip pans into gore, a Christmas episode that is genuinely nasty. Season two swaps Christmas for Easter and pushes further than it needs to, which is the whole appeal and also why it lost people.

**4/5.** Cancelled after two seasons, which is roughly right — it was never going to sustain a third at that volume.
