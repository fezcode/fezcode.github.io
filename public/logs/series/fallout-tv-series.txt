## About

**Fallout (2024–)** is Prime Video's adaptation of Bethesda's post-apocalyptic games, developed by Geneva Robertson-Dworet and Graham Wagner with Jonathan Nolan and Lisa Joy producing. It follows Lucy MacLean (Ella Purnell), a relentlessly polite Vault Dweller who climbs out into the irradiated wasteland of Los Angeles two hundred years after the bombs, and runs into Maximus (Aaron Moten) of the Brotherhood of Steel and The Ghoul (Walton Goggins), a centuries-old bounty hunter who used to be a Hollywood cowboy. [Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallout_(American_TV_series))

It gets the tone exactly right: the retro-futurist Vault-Tec optimism, the dark jokes, the sudden violence, and the cheerful 50s music playing over all of it. It does not retell any of the games. It tells a new story inside the same world, and it feels like a Fallout game you are watching instead of playing.

Then the first season ends by pointing at New Vegas, and the second season goes there. As someone who loved *Fallout: New Vegas*, seeing the Mojave on screen — the Strip, the factions, the whole thing — made me genuinely happy.

**5/5.** A great TV show, and the rare game adaptation that clearly comes from people who love the source material.
