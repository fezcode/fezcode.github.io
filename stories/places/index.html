Glareach

City

Once a poor town in Thornus, destroyed in the War of Fourbournes, its center rebuilt as a neon-soaked metropolis of casinos and vice. A monument to Aester Cloudwear's greed. Only what could turn a profit was rebuilt: the old districts and the rocky coastline remain ash-choked ruins, left to the ghosts.

Predymesh

Capital City

The capital city of Thornus. A place of gilded spires and deep corruption, where the air is rich and the morals are poor. Home to the ruling class and the shadow ruler, Wulf Kosnak. Gas lamps burn beside neon, and carriages share the cobbles with growling motorcars; in Predymesh, the century you live in depends on the street you can afford.

Thornus

Country

A nation of sharp contrasts, where ancient traditions are suffocated by modern corruption. From the neon vice of Glareach to the gilded spires of Predymesh, it is a land held together by the iron grip of shadow rulers. Nominally a kingdom—its crowned figurehead signs where Wulf Kosnak points—its cities are run by mayors who exist to absorb the blame. Technology pools where money pools: satellite eyes and motorcars for the ministries and the studios, gas lamps and carriage-lines for everyone else.
