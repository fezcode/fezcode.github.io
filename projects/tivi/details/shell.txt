## Built like a component, not a web app

A borderless resizable window with rounded corners and a shadow — Win11 DWM on one side, a macOS layer on the other — aero snap, fullscreen, always-on-top, a right-click context menu and a pinnable control bar.

Brightness, contrast, saturation, hue and gamma are applied live on the GPU. Five color themes ship, plus an **Adaptive** theme that tints the whole interface from the playing video, the way Timp tints itself from album art.

Volume boost amplifies quiet media in software up to 200%, with a notch at 100% so you can find your way back.

## Installed properly, on both platforms

**Windows** — `tivi-Setup-<version>.exe` is fully self-contained: FFmpeg, libass and raylib bundled, registered in Add/Remove Programs, with its own uninstaller and a silent `/S` switch.

**macOS (arm64)** — `tivi-<version>-macos-arm64.zip` unzips to a `tivi.app` with the same libraries bundled, no Homebrew required. Finder "Open With" works once it sits in `/Applications`.

System-wide media keys, single-instance file handoff, snapshots to the Desktop, and settings that autosave as you change them round it out.
