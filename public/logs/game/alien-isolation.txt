# Alien: Isolation

Alien: Isolation is the best Alien story since the original film. The Xenomorph is not scripted; it hunts you, learns from you, and the motion tracker's beep becomes the most stressful sound in gaming.

The retro-futuristic art direction, the chunky CRT monitors and the clanking machinery make Sevastopol feel completely real. Hiding in a locker holding your breath has never been so intense.
