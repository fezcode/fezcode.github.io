# Cogas
One library for every store.
(version) 0.15.2

**Cogas** — *common gaming software* — collects the games already installed on this PC across **Steam, Epic, GOG, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net, EA and the Microsoft Store**, plus anything you point it at yourself, into a single cover grid. Every one of them launches through its own platform. It also searches every storefront at once, compares prices, tracks a wishlist, and puts critic scores on the art.

:::spec
Yield: one library, seven stores
Prep: ~30 seconds, no accounts
Difficulty: beginner — run the installer
Pantry: Windows only
:::
