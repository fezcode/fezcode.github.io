Preview is not a second-class view. Tags, lists and mid-line styling all render in place, and find/replace searches the rendered text in preview and the raw source in edit mode — so the highlights line up either way.

## Images in the preview pane

Image files in the vault sidebar open straight into the preview, and embeds render inline alongside the prose.

![Image embeds in preview](/images/projects/descry/images.png)

## Typographic math

Inline `$…$` and display `$$…$$` spans render as real Unicode glyphs — Greek letters, operators, `\frac{a}{b}`, roots, and simple super/subscripts:

```text
$\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i^2$   →   ∑ᵢ₌₁ⁿ xᵢ²
```

This is a typographic pass, not a full TeX engine — which is the honest trade for a native binary that starts instantly.

## Everything reachable from the keyboard

- **Quick switcher** (`Ctrl+P`) — recents-first, fuzzy match.
- **Command palette** (`Ctrl+Shift+P`) — every action plus every Lua-registered plugin action, with a category chip and its bound shortcut.
- **Outline panel** auto-generated from headings, pinned or modal.
- **Vault-wide search** across every note with a results overlay.
- **Go menu** — Go to File, Line, Heading and Tag, plus "Last edit" to hop back to where you were typing.
