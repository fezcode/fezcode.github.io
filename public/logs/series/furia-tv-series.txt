## About

**Furia (2025–)**, released in English as *Rage*, is a Spanish black comedy for HBO Max, created, written and directed by Félix Sabroso. Over eight half-hour episodes it follows five women — Carmen Machi, Candela Peña, Cecilia Roth, Nathalie Poza and Pilar Castro — each pushed past their limit by some everyday injustice or manipulation, and each deciding to take revenge. [Wikipedia overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rage_(2025_TV_series))

On paper it has everything: a heavyweight cast of Spanish actresses sharing the screen for the first time, a sharp premise, and a satirical bite that refuses to spare even its own heroines. The cast is reliably good.

In practice it did not land for me. The anger is the whole point, but the satire stays loud and broad rather than cutting, and the episodes feel more like a string of sketches than a story building towards something.

**2/5.** Great actresses, a promising idea, not much that stuck.
