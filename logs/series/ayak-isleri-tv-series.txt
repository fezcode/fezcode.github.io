## About

**Ayak İşleri (2021–2024)**, released in English as *Running Errands*, is a Turkish comedy series directed by Caner Özyurtlu and written by Özyurtlu, Volkan Öge and Deniz Alnıtemiz, produced by AC Film for GAİN. Four seasons and thirty-eight episodes follow Vedat (Çağlar Çorumlu) and Evren (Güven Murat Akpınar), two men sent out by a wealthy businessman to run his "extremely important" errands. [Vikipedi](https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayak_%C4%B0%C5%9Fleri)

## Why It Works

The engine is the pairing. Vedat is the seasoned fixer who closes his eyes and does the job; Evren studied philosophy, cannot stop asking whether the job should be done at all, and has to learn the trade anyway. Each episode sends them out on a new errand, but the real friction is never the task — it's the two of them, arguing about it the whole way there.

That gives the show a simple, sturdy shape: a self-contained mission, a comedy of two worldviews grinding against each other, and just enough of Vedat's buried history to keep it from being only jokes. Çorumlu and Akpınar are the reason it holds together; the rhythm between them does more work than any single plot.

**4/5.** A mismatched-duo comedy that knows exactly what it is and plays it well.
