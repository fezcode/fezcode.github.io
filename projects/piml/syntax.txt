LABEL: Syntax
SUBTEXT: The Grammar

In a world of configuration hell, PIML offers a sanctuary of clarity. Our architecture prioritizes human vertical scanning and structural clarity over machine-centric verbosity.

- **No Quotes**: Keys and simple strings don't need them — `"..."` is there when you want to force a string.
- **No Trailing Commas**: End of line means end of value.
- **Explicit Structure**: Parentheses (key) clearly denote fields.
- **Lists**: Denoted by > for clear, vertical scanning — and they nest.
- **Comments**: Native # support, full-line and inline; a glued # stays literal, so URLs need no escaping.
- **Multi-line Strings**: Indentation-based text blocks, with interior blank lines preserved.
- **Strict Indentation**: Exactly two spaces per level. Tabs and skipped levels are errors, not guesses.

PIML is designed to be the "Anti-JSON" — removing the noise and keeping the signal. It's not just a format; it's a way of organizing thought.
